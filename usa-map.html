🇺🇸 vs 🇧🇪 The USA and Flanders side by side

Both systems take children from about age 3 to 18 and then on to higher education, but the names, the tracks and the exams are different. Here is a rough map between them.

Watch the false friends! In the US, a public school is a free state school (in Britain it can mean a private one). College usually means university (not a Belgian college = secondary school). A student can be any pupil, and to graduate means to finish a level of school, not only university.
