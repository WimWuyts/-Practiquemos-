FREEZE FRAME

WATCH · FREEZE · REPORT

Every scene is calm and busy — people and animals doing things: eating, working, playing, flying. Watch carefully what everyone is doing.

Then, suddenly, something happens and the whole scene freezes. Your job: report what everyone was doing when it happened — past continuous meets simple past.

Pick a scene at the top of the page. Start with The Last Day or choose another.
