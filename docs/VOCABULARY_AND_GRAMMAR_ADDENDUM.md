## Grammar

### The verb 'be' · A 'be' (lenni) ige

Use am with I, is with he/she/it, are with you/we/they. Short forms: I'm, you're, he's.

_Az I mellett am, a he/she/it mellett is, a you/we/they mellett are áll. Rövid alakok: I'm, you're, he's._

- I am Marta. — Marta vagyok.
- She is my niece. — Ő az unokahúgom.
- We are from Hungary. — Magyarországról jövünk.

### Subject pronouns · Alanyi névmások

I, you, he, she, it, we, they replace names. Use he for a man, she for a woman.

_Az I, you, he, she, it, we, they a nevek helyett áll. He = férfi, she = nő._

- He is Spanish. — Ő spanyol.
- They live in Denmark. — Ők Dániában élnek.

### Question words · Kérdőszavak

What asks about things, Where about places, How about the way. The verb comes after the question word.

_A what dolgokra, a where helyre, a how módra kérdez. A kérdőszó után jön az ige._

- What is your name? — Hogy hívnak?
- Where are you from? — Honnan jössz?
- How are you? — Hogy vagy?

### a / an · a / an (egy)

Use a before a consonant sound, an before a vowel sound: a teacher, an apple.

_Mássalhangzó előtt a, magánhangzó előtt an: a teacher, an apple._

- I am a teacher. — Tanár vagyok.
- It is an apple. — Ez egy alma.

### Possessive adjectives · Birtokos névmások

my, your, his, her, our, their show who owns something. His for a man, her for a woman.

_A my, your, his, her, our, their megmutatja, kié valami. His = férfié, her = nőé._

- This is my family. — Ez az én családom.
- Her name is Kira. — Az ő neve Kira.

### Possessive 's · Birtokos 's

Add 's to a name: Kira's boyfriend = the boyfriend of Kira.

_Tegyél 's-t a név után: Kira's boyfriend = Kira barátja._

- Endika is Kira's boyfriend. — Endika Kira barátja.
- This is Marta's house. — Ez Marta háza.

### have / has · have / has (van neki)

Use have with I/you/we/they and has with he/she/it.

_Az I/you/we/they mellett have, a he/she/it mellett has áll._

- I have two sisters. — Két nővérem van.
- She has a nice house. — Szép háza van.

### Plural nouns · Többes szám

Add -s for more than one: one sister, two sisters. Some words change: child → children.

_Több dologhoz tegyél -s-t: one sister, two sisters. Néhány szó változik: child → children._

- I have three brothers. — Három bátyám van.
- Two coffees, please. — Két kávét kérek.

### Present simple · Egyszerű jelen

Use the base verb: I get up, they work. Add -s for he/she/it: she works.

_Alap ige: I get up, they work. He/she/it mellett -s: she works._

- I get up at seven. — Hétkor kelek.
- She works in Belgium. — Belgiumban dolgozik.

### do / does questions · do / does kérdések

Use Do you...? and Does he/she...? Do not add -s to the main verb: Does she work?

_Do you...? és Does he/she...? A fő igéhez ne tegyél -s-t: Does she work?_

- Do you like coffee? — Szereted a kávét?
- Does she live here? — Itt lakik?

### always, usually, sometimes, never · gyakorisági határozók

These words usually come before the main verb: I always drink coffee.

_Ezek a szavak általában a fő ige előtt állnak: I always drink coffee._

- I usually get up early. — Általában korán kelek.
- She never eats meat. — Soha nem eszik húst.

### there is / there are · there is / there are (van / vannak)

There is for one thing, there are for more than one: There is a garden. There are two rooms.

_Egy dologhoz there is, többhöz there are: There is a garden. There are two rooms._

- There is a kitchen. — Van egy konyha.
- There are two bedrooms. — Két hálószoba van.

### some / any · some / any

Use some in positive sentences, any in questions and negatives: I have some bread. Do you have any milk?

_Állításban some, kérdésben/tagadásban any: I have some bread. Do you have any milk?_

- I'd like some water. — Kérnék egy kis vizet.
- Is there any coffee? — Van kávé?

### I'd like · Kérnék (I'd like)

I'd like = I would like. It is more polite than I want.

_I'd like = I would like. Udvariasabb, mint az I want._

- I'd like a coffee, please. — Kérnék egy kávét.
- I'd like the soup. — A levest kérném.

### can / can't · can / can't (tud / nem tud)

Can + base verb: I can swim. Can't = cannot. Questions: Can you help me?

_Can + alap ige: I can swim. Can't = cannot. Kérdés: Can you help me?_

- I can cook. — Tudok főzni.
- Can you help me? — Tudsz segíteni?

### going to (plans) · going to (tervek)

be + going to + verb: I am going to visit Kira.

_be + going to + ige: I am going to visit Kira._

- I'm going to travel to Prague. — Prágába fogok utazni.
- We are going to cook dinner. — Vacsorát fogunk főzni.

### Present continuous · Folyamatos jelen

be + verb+ing for actions happening now: I am cooking. She is working.

_be + ige+ing a most történő cselekvésre: I am cooking. She is working._

- I am reading a book. — Könyvet olvasok éppen.
- They are watching a film. — Filmet néznek éppen.

### at / on / in (time) · at / on / in (idő)

at for times (at seven), on for days (on Monday), in for months (in July).

_at időpontra (at seven), on napra (on Monday), in hónapra (in July)._

- The lesson is at ten. — Az óra tízkor van.
- I'm free on Saturday. — Szombaton ráérek.

### Object pronouns · Tárgyi névmások

me, you, him, her, it, us, them come after the verb: Can you hear me?

_me, you, him, her, it, us, them az ige után áll: Can you hear me?_

- Can you hear me? — Hallasz engem?
- I like him. — Kedvelem őt.

### like + -ing · like + -ing

like/love/enjoy + verb+ing: I like cooking. She loves reading.

_like/love/enjoy + ige+ing: I like cooking. She loves reading._

- I love cooking. — Imádok főzni.
- He enjoys swimming. — Élvez úszni.

### Imperatives · Felszólítás

Use the base verb alone: Turn left. Wait here. Add please to be polite.

_Csak az alap ige: Turn left. Wait here. Udvariasan: tegyél please-t._

- Turn right at the corner. — A saroknál fordulj jobbra.
- Please wait a moment. — Kérlek, várj egy pillanatot.

### Could you...? / Could I...? · Could you...? / Could I...?

Could you help me? asks someone to do something. Could I have...? asks for something for yourself.

_Could you help me? = kérsz vkit vmire. Could I have...? = kérsz magadnak vmit._

- Could you help me, please? — Tudna segíteni, kérem?
- Could I have some water? — Kaphatnék egy kis vizet?

### was / were · was / were (volt)

was with I/he/she/it, were with you/we/they: I was at home. They were happy.

_was: I/he/she/it, were: you/we/they. I was at home. They were happy._

- I was at my sister's house. — A nővérem házában voltam.
- The weather was nice. — Szép idő volt.

### Past simple · Egyszerű múlt

Add -ed to many verbs: visited, cooked. Some change: go → went, have → had, see → saw.

_Sok igéhez -ed: visited, cooked. Néhány változik: go → went, have → had, see → saw._

- I visited my sister. — Meglátogattam a nővéremet.
- We went to Prague. — Prágába mentünk.

### need to / have to · need to / have to (kell)

need to / have to + base verb: I need to pack. I have to check in.

_need to / have to + alap ige: I need to pack. I have to check in._

- I need to buy a ticket. — Jegyet kell vennem.
- We have to be early. — Korán kell odaérnünk.

### Comparing: -er / more · Összehasonlítás: -er / more

Short words add -er: older, bigger. Long words use more: more interesting. Use 'than' to compare.

_Rövid szóhoz -er: older, bigger. Hosszú szóhoz more: more interesting. A 'than' köti össze._

- My sister is older than me. — A nővérem idősebb nálam.
- This book is more interesting. — Ez a könyv érdekesebb.

### and, but, because · and, but, because

and adds, but shows a difference, because gives a reason.

_and hozzáad, but ellentétet mutat, because okot ad._

- I like tea but I don't like coffee. — Szeretem a teát, de a kávét nem.
- I'm happy because you're here. — Boldog vagyok, mert itt vagy.
